# English frequency data

`en-frequency.txt` is the unmodified `content/2018/en/en_50k.txt` from
https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords (Hermit Dave), retrieved 2026-09-07.
Underlying corpus: OpenSubtitles2018 / OPUS, https://opus.nlpl.eu/OpenSubtitles2018.php.

**The data is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0**, as stated in the project's README:
https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords/blob/master/README.md#license.
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/.
`FREQUENCY-LICENSE.txt` is the upstream MIT license for its generator CODE, not the data license.

The application uses word rank as a transparent approximate familiarity indicator.
It is not a CEFR-certified lexicon or a collocation-frequency corpus. Subtitle language is biased
towards conversation, film dialogue and names; a missing word does not prove rarity.
No corpus material is sent to Gemini as a full dataset.
